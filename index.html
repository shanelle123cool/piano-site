Learn a little more every day!

🎹 The Notes

The seven musical note names are C, D, E, F, G, A and B. Then the pattern repeats.

🎵 The White Keys

White keys play natural notes. Black keys play sharps or flats, depending on the musical context.

🎶 Rhythm

Whole notes, half notes, quarter notes and eighth notes have different durations.

🎼 Chords

A chord is a group of notes played together. Chords help accompany melodies.

🌟 Piano Fact

A standard full-size piano has 88 keys: 52 white keys and 36 black keys.
